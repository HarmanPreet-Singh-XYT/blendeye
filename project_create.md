# Project Creation Guide — The Vault Protocol

Follow this step-by-step preset to build and generate a brand new movie project from scratch, testing the full Gemini screenplay generation → Perspective Sharder → ClickHouse time-gate pipeline.

---

### Step 1 — Concept & Directorial Vision

- **Title:** The Vault Protocol
- **Logline:** A three-person heist crew breaches a private bank's sub-basement vault. The demolitions expert believes their exit route is secure — she doesn't know the getaway driver has already been paid off to seal it.
- **Genre:** Thriller (pick a card in that category — e.g. "Heist Thriller" if present, otherwise any Thriller card)
- **Director Style:** Michael Mann (his cold, procedural style fits a heist cleanly)
- **Narrative Format:** short (18 min / 6 scenes typical — doesn't matter, generator always makes 3-4 scenes, but this keeps runtime/scrubber numbers sane)
- **Target Runtime:** leave default (~18 min) or set to 15
- **Scene 1 Placement:** leave default (Act I Hook, 5%)

---

### Step 2 — Character Lab

*(Use exactly 3 characters, keep names short and easy to say on camera)*

1. **Character 1**
   - **Name:** Rae
   - **Dramatic Role:** Lead Protagonist
   - **Archetype & Motivation:** Veteran demolitions expert, meticulous, trusts her crew completely
   - **Speech Style:** terse, technical, controlled
   - **Subtext Level:** moderate

2. **Character 2**
   - **Name:** Kessler
   - **Dramatic Role:** Strategic Foil-Antagonist
   - **Archetype & Motivation:** Getaway driver secretly bought out by a rival crew; needs the job to fail without anyone tracing it to him
   - **Speech Style:** calm, reassuring, overly agreeable
   - **Subtext Level:** high

3. **Character 3**
   - **Name:** Priya
   - **Dramatic Role:** Inside Informant
   - **Archetype & Motivation:** Bank security consultant feeding the crew real-time floor intel
   - **Speech Style:** clipped, professional
   - **Subtext Level:** moderate

---

### Step 3 — Staging & Secret

- **Primary Location:** Sub-basement vault, First Continental Bank
- **Core Secret (this is the important one):** Kessler has been paid by a rival crew to seal the vault's exit corridor once Rae and Priya are inside, trapping them so the rival crew can claim the score. Rae does not know Kessler has betrayed them until the exit corridor is sealed.
- **Shoot Region:** any preset city, doesn't matter for demo
- **Budget Fields:** leave at defaults

---

### Step 4 — Target Territories

Pick any 1-2, irrelevant to the demo (e.g. US, UK).

---

### Step 5 — Review & Generate

1. Review the configured fields in the final step.
2. Click **Generate**.
3. Once generated, navigate to the **Simulation / Hot Seat** tab to test character knowledge before and after the corridor is sealed.
